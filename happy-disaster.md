# Happy Disaster — Rule Sheet

## Objective
Reach 10 points first, or lead after seven rounds.

## Secret choices
Each round, both players choose one action and lock it before the choices are resolved.

- **Build:** score 2 points and regain 1 energy (maximum 5).
- **Dash:** score 3 on calm rounds, 1 on slippery rounds, or 2 on wild rounds.
- **Redirect:** score 1; if the opponent Dashes, gain 2 bonus points and cancel their Dash points.
- If both players Redirect, both receive a small bonus.

## Hazard cycle
Round 1 is calm, round 2 slippery, round 3 wild, then repeat.

## Fair-play rule
The game never insults a player for losing. The recap describes choices and points, not personal traits.
