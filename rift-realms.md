# Rift Realms — Rule Sheet

## Objective
Control the most tiles when the 20-turn match limit is reached.

## Setup
Use a 5×5 board. Blue begins with pieces on cells 1 and 5. Pink begins on cells 21 and 25. Cells 5, 21, and the opposite corners act as portal-linked positions as implemented in the prototype.

## Turn
1. Select one of your pieces.
2. Select an empty orthogonally adjacent tile, or use a portal link.
3. The destination becomes your territory.
4. Turns alternate.

## End
The match ends after 20 turns or when the current player has no legal move. The player controlling more tiles wins; equal territory is a draw.

## Strategy
Preserve access to multiple empty neighbors, avoid trapping both pieces, and use portal links to switch sides quickly.
